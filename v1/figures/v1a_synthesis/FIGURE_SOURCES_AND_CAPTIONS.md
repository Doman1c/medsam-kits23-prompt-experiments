# V1a synthesis figure sources and captions

All reported values are frozen synthesis values. No retraining, threshold tuning, expansion tuning, or test-set model selection was performed for this figure pack.

## Main table

Main validation ablation from V1a.1 through V1a.6. V1a.2-A has the highest historical validation case-volume Dice (`0.855560`); V1a.3-B B1 remains the final frozen robust operating point (`all-slice=0.835611`, `case-volume=0.854752`, `negative specificity=0.848954`).

## Figure 1 — MedSAM box robustness

Panel A: Dice retention under center shift and corner jitter. Panel B: Dice retention under box scale. Moderate box errors are tolerated, while sufficiently large geometry perturbations sharply degrade frozen-MedSAM performance.

## Figure 2 — Oracle gap + architecture

Panel A: V1a.1 automatic/oracle decomposition. Panel B: final frozen V1a architecture: 2.5D CT → lightweight bilateral prompt network → left/right boxes → V1a.3-B case-level single-span decoder → 1.20× expansion → frozen MedSAM.

## Figure 3 — Quantitative z-continuity effect

Locked V1a.3-A B0 versus frozen V1a.3-B B1 on validation. The principal gains are all-slice Dice and negative specificity, with only a small positive-Dice change. This aggregate panel is used instead of the earlier proposed `case_00053` per-slice visualization because the required per-slice probability source table is not part of the frozen compact repository artifacts.

## Figure 4 — `case_00067` qualitative failure progression

Four validation slices (`z=19,21,22,24`) from the remote left-kidney satellite are shown. B1 removes all four true-positive slices and produces no prompt. R2b restores presence, but frozen-MedSAM Dice is `0.357, 0.076, 0.000, 0.000`; G6 changes the geometry and gives `0.358, 0.028, 0.018, 0.000`. Green contours denote ground truth, cyan contours frozen-MedSAM predictions, and red rectangles the 1.20× expanded prompt boxes. The masks were generated by a visualization-only re-forward using the frozen evaluator logic on these four validation slices; no selection or tuning was performed.

## Figure 5 — Prompt metric / MedSAM mismatch

Frozen V1a.6 Stage-1 and Stage-2 aggregate values. G6 improves tight-box IoU, center error, coverage, and the prompt-success audit, yet recovered-slice MedSAM Dice decreases and false-positive pixels increase.

## Figure 6 — Failure progression

Schematic of the measured bottleneck → controlled intervention → next bottleneck sequence from V0 through V1a.6. It documents why the image-only patch sequence stops rather than continuing to V1a.7.
